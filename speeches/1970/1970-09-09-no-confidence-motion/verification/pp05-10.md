## English-translation impact

Most corrections above are orthographic or spacing corrections and do not alter the existing English meaning. Two page-9 corrections require an English adjustment during the translation re-check:

1. The source explicitly places the first cited electoral victory at `ஆறுவது மாதத்தில்`; the translation should preserve that temporal statement rather than omit it.
2. The restored `ஏழெட்டு இடங்கள் தான் முன்பு இருந்தன` must appear in English: the speech contrasts the earlier seven or eight municipal councils with the later figure of more than fifty.
