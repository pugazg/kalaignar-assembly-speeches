## Transcription policy

1. Preserve the wording and spelling printed in the source. Do not silently modernise Tamil.
2. Preserve printed section headings.
3. Preserve parliamentary speaker labels and interjections where printed.
4. Preserve numerals and monetary figures as printed.
5. Normalise only physical line-wraps caused by typesetting; paragraph structure is retained.
6. Mark every printed/source-page transition with an HTML comment such as `<!-- source-page: 5 -->`.
7. Where a character or word cannot be read confidently from the scan, mark it explicitly for review rather than guessing.
8. The English portion is a faithful translation placed **after** the complete Tamil transcription; it is not treated as source text.
