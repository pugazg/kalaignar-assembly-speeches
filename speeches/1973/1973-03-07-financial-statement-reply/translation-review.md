### Correction 1 — scan p.5 → p.6: preserve the source level of agency

Gate F:
`was among those who had me locked up ... and thereby helped the Assembly to be conducted here`

The verified Tamil uses `துணையாக இருந்தவர்கள்`, which supports complicity/support but not the stronger English causative `had me locked up`.

### Correction 2 — scan p.7: restore the Finance Commission proposition

Gate F incorrectly negated the source by saying:

`I have not said that the Finance Commission itself must calculate and recommend all expenditure on Plans.`

The verified Tamil instead states that the Finance Commission itself should take Plan expenditure into account and make recommendations, while the Planning Commission may be assigned the year-to-year assistance role under principles laid down by the Finance Commission.

Gate G restored that proposition and retained the following sentence that the Planning Commission is not being denied every role in allocation.

### Correction 3 — scan p.8: remove an unsupported Planning Commission negation

Gate F said:

`the Planning Commission should perform no such function`

The verified Tamil does not contain that `no such function` proposition. Gate G replaced it with a conservative rendering of the source's compressed wording and did not attempt to harmonise it with outside administrative practice.

### Correction 4 — scan p.9: health-budget terminology

Gate F rendered `மக்கள் நல் வாழ்வு` as `public welfare`.

Gate G changed it to `public health`, which is the source-supported term in this immediate health-budget discussion.
