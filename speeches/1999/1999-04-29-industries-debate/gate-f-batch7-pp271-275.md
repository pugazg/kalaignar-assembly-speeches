### Source page 273

There is a case relating to what occurred in the granting of granite leases. This Government has already filed a case before the Special Court concerning corruption in granite leases. The proceedings in that case are under way. In the meantime, the former Industries Minister accused in that case went to the High Court and filed a petition asking that the case itself be quashed. After hearing that petition, I wish to point out here what judgment the High Court judge delivered. How was that order issued? On the basis of what order did that wrongdoing take place? There was a rule called Rule 39. Through Government Order No. 97, Industries Department, dated 8-3-1993, the previous Government created a new Rule 39 in the Tamil Nadu Minor Mineral Rules and granted quarry leases to persons of its choice. Although, under an order issued by the Industries Department of the Kazhagam Government in 1989, there were opportunities for the Government to receive revenue — this must be noted — although under the order we had issued there were opportunities for substantial revenue to come to the Government, although many cases concerning the granting of licences were pending before the High Court, although lessees had submitted letters expressing willingness to pay the amount demanded by the Government — all the lessees who had taken part in the auction had submitted letters expressing willingness — although the rules required prior approval of the Central Government for granting licences on Forest Department land, even violating that rule, although the then Advocate General and the Chief Secretary to Government had raised objections and advised against it, and although it had been recorded in the file that the Government had an opportunity to receive revenue of Rs. 12 crore from just one lease—
