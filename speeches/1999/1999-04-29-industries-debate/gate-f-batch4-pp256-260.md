### Source page 256

It was during this period that the Alangulam cement factory too was established. I inform the House that the SIPCOT organisation that we created was of great use for all these things. In 1973, there was a tract of 729 acres at Ranipet. When we assumed office, a decision had already been taken that this land had been purchased during the Congress Government, that it was of no use for anything, and that it could therefore be sold. The then Industries Minister Madhavan and I went to Ranipet and inspected that place. It was then that we raised the question: why not create SIPCOT and establish a complex here? And we established that complex at Ranipet at that time. In that complex, 107 new industries were started with an investment of Rs. 168 crore.

After that, in 1974, a second industrial complex was established by the Kazhagam Government at Hosur over an area of 1,236 acres. In it, 186 factories were started with an investment of Rs. 500 crore. I am happy to inform the House that, in the 293 industries started in the two industrial complexes at Ranipet and Hosur, nearly 20,000 people have obtained employment.

If we look at the details of factories established after 1989, from 1989 to 1990, it was in 1989-90 that an industrial undertaking called “Asian Paints” was started with an investment of Rs. 22 crore.

The company “Tele Communications Consultant India Limited” was started with an investment of Rs. 29 crore.
