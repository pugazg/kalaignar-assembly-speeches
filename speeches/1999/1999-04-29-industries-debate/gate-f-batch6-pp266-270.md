### Source page 268

CDR means the extent to which banks increase their lending. It depends on their capacity. At the same time, it also depends on all of these: the infrastructure of those banks, the capacity to recover loans in that area, the nature of support for investment, and adequate facilities for recovering bank loans. That is what CDR is. These statistics explain that in this too we are in the leading position.

### Source page 269

I have referred many times in this House to all the efforts undertaken by this Government to expand this information-technology centre in Tamil Nadu. The members of the House know very well that information technology, which in the seventies was regarded merely as a conceptual form, has today become something that is present everywhere. Every branch of industrial development is being intertwined with and developed by information technology. Information technology is becoming something integrated with the world economy. Any industry without information technology is now regarded as an old industry that has not progressed.

We know that information technology is going to serve as the driving force of the world economy in the 21st century. That is why, for the growth of information technology and so that its benefits may penetrate rural areas and reach ordinary people too, the Government has been taking various measures with a long-term vision.

Of the 23,000 engineering graduates newly coming out each year in Tamil Nadu, more than 13,000 graduates are now coming out after receiving education in information technology and related fields.

It is in Tamil Nadu that the largest number of software-engineering professionals in India are working. At present, more than 22,000 people in Tamil Nadu have software as their livelihood — Software Professionals.

For information technology itself, a separate department has been created in the Chennai Secretariat under the leadership of an I.A.S. officer and has been functioning from 5-10-1998.

### Source page 270

The members know that, for information technology to develop rapidly in Tamil Nadu and for approaches to it to be formulated and implemented, an I.T. Task Force has been created under the leadership of the Chief Minister.

The Government has formulated and announced a separate policy (I.T.Policy) for information technology. This policy has received a good response among information-technology entrepreneurs.

At Siruseri on the Old Mahabalipuram Road, over an area of 1,000 acres, SIPCOT is developing an information-technology park of international standard for large-scale hardware (Hardware) and software (Software) development. I also inform the House that the stretch in Chennai from Taramani to the Old Mahabalipuram Road is rapidly developing as an information-technology highway (I.T. Super Highway).

Recently, Tata Consultancy Services started Asia's largest software centre at Sholinganallur. Following it, other very large companies — Wipro, Infosys and Polaris — are establishing their centres in that area.

I have already stated that, through that major undertaking we are to carry out in connection with the “Worldtel” organisation, employment will be available to one lakh fifty thousand people throughout Tamil Nadu. Whichever organisation it may ultimately be through, I inform the House that this Government, with your support, will expedite it and demonstrate both the provision of employment to one lakh fifty thousand people and that scientific innovation by which the whole of Tamil Nadu becomes internet-enabled.
