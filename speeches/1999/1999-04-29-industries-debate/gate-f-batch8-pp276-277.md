".... the money due to the Government has been siphoned off by them and as a result of which they had pecuniary advantage to the extent of several crores."

The situation in which such a Government existed has changed, and this Government is now functioning in this industrial sector with your affectionate support and the affectionate support of the general public. (Sound of desk-thumping.)

To encourage the granite industry further, we have now changed the rules, brought in an amendment called 8-A, and introduced methods of auctioning. Even in that, this Government is considering how still greater revenue may be obtained. I have referred, in my reply during the debate on the Budget, to various measures for managing all our present deficits.

To encourage the granite industry further, there is now land belonging to ‘TAMIN’, is there not? The Government is making efforts to take a portion of that land and put it to public auction — instead of TAMIN itself keeping it and sleeping over it, leaving it idle — and, by putting even that ‘TAMIN’ land to public auction, to seek revenue for the Government through that as well. Conveying this, and thanking all the members who, from the morning until now, have offered valuable views and encouraged me, I conclude. Vanakkam. (Sound of desk-thumping.)

**Hon. Speaker:** Hon. Leader of the Opposition.

**Thiru So. Balakrishnan:** Hon. Speaker, I wish to state here that the salt-pan industry in our Tamil Nadu is in a dangerous situation—

—in my district, salt pans are operating over more than 5,000 acres. Under the previous Government, the lease amount had been Rs. 29 per acre; because it was raised to Rs. 429, all the private parties have today obtained stays from the courts. At the same time, because the salt-pan industry run by the Government is under an obligation to pay Rs. 1½ crore to the Government, there is a danger that the industry may have to close. I have already spoken about this in the Budget. Our Chief Minister should create a favourable situation for this and restore it to the old rate of Rs. 29 itself. Nowhere else in India is it this high. What had been Rs. 29 has been raised to Rs. 429 as the lease per acre. I request our Chief Minister to cancel this large amount and help the salt-pan industry continue permanently and enable thousands of workers to work.

**Hon. Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi:** Hon. Speaker, this Government will not disregard the good suggestion made by the Leader of the Opposition. If it has been raised to around Rs. 400, that is indeed high. Even if it is not possible to reduce it to the old level of Rs. 29, I inform the House that the Government will consider reducing it to a level that the Leader of the Opposition and the others can agree to. Not only the salt-pan (*uppalam*) industry; this Government will also ensure that the *appalam* industry in Tamil Nadu does not suffer. (Sound of desk-thumping.)
