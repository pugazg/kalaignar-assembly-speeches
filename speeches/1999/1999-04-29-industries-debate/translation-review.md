### Definite correction applied

Source p.245 contains the idiomatic phrase `நீங்கள் பெயர்தட்டிக் கொண்டு போகிறீர்கள்`. Gate F had rendered this over-literally as:

- `You are taking the nameplate and going away with it.`

Gate G corrected it to:

- `You are taking the credit for it.`

This preserves the meaning of the Tamil accusation in context rather than treating `பெயர்தட்டிக்` as a literal physical nameplate.
