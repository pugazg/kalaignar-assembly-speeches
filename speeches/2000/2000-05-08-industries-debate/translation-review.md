## Definite Gate-G corrections

### 1. Source p.284 — restore Kalaignar's actual argumentative transition

The Gate-F text had replaced the verified Tamil transition after the Internet/scientific-development passage with unsupported generic prose saying that information technology had become the driving force of the world economy.

The verified Tamil instead moves from the extent of scientific development to Kalaignar's argument that **we must create a situation in which we can join it and compete with it**, immediately followed by his assertion that everyone and the newspapers praise Tamil Nadu as having attained first place in India.

Gate G restored that sequence. This correction is significant for voice fidelity because the first-pass version sounded plausible in English but substituted a generic explanatory paragraph for Kalaignar's own argumentative movement.

### 2. Source p.286 — preserve the source-sensitive camera wording

The verified Tamil contains the unusual wording `இந்தக் கேமிரா கழுவும்போது`. Gate F had interpretively rendered this as `When this camera develops`.

Gate G changed it to the deliberately source-close `When this camera is washed`, rather than silently normalising the unusual source expression. The surrounding camera/light joke and Subbarayan exchange remain intact.
