## Archival scope

This folder is the reader-facing Tamil/English location for the `நமது நிலை` source package.

The booklet is an **edited two-House compilation**, not one continuous verbatim Assembly speech. Its two printed units contain material associated with replies delivered in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council. This entry therefore preserves the booklet **as printed** rather than reconstructing separate House transcripts.

The publication date above is a booklet date, not a single speech date; accordingly `metadata.json` keeps `date: null`.
