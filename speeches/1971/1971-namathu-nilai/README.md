## Archival scope

This folder is the reader-facing speech/translation location for the `நமது நிலை` source package.

The booklet is an **edited two-House compilation**, not one continuous verbatim Assembly speech. Its two printed units contain material associated with replies delivered in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council.

For that reason, this folder preserves the booklet **as printed** rather than reconstructing separate House transcripts.

## Non-negotiable textual-authority rule

The only textual authority is:

`ACL-CPL_01726_நமது_நிலை.pdf`

`../../../sources/1971-namathu-nilai/transcription/`

Other Assembly/Council PDFs are reference/provenance sources only. Their wording must not be copied into, merged with, or used to repair the Tamil text or English translation here.
