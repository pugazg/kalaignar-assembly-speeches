## Archival scope

This folder is the reader-facing location for the booklet witness.

The source introduction says the booklet compiles replies delivered on:

- **29-6-1971** in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly;
- **30-6-1971** in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council.

The body itself is printed as one continuous editorial unit under `நமது விளக்கம்`, scans **4–60**, without a securely printed internal House divider. This entry therefore preserves the booklet **as printed** and does not invent two page ranges or reconstruct separate House transcripts.

Because the booklet has more than one underlying speech date, `metadata.json` keeps `date: null`.
