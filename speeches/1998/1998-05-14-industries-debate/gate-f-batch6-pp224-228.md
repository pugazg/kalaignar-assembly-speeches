### Source page 224

‘Saint-Gobain’ glass factory. An agreement to establish this factory with the French ‘Saint-Gobain’ glass company was entered into on 6-1-1997. This glass factory, to be established with an investment of Rs. 450 crore, is being set up in the Sriperumbudur Industrial Development Park. It will begin production by the end of 1999.

P.V.C. Rexine factory. The project report for establishing, in the joint sector at the Ennore Petroleum Industrial Complex, the P.V.C. factory project of the Denmark-based ‘North Cyto’ company has been submitted to TIDCO. This factory will produce the raw materials required for manufacturing 1 lakh 50 thousand tonnes of P.V.C. products annually. The investment in this project is Rs. 350 crore. The cooperation agreement for this project was entered into on 10-3-1997.

Bis-Phenol-A chemical factory. Modi Corp of New Delhi, together with TIDCO, is to start in Cuddalore district a factory capable of producing 40 thousand tonnes a year of the chemical Bis-Phenol-A. The promoters’ agreement for this project was signed on 16-7-1997. With an investment of Rs. 250 crore, this factory will provide direct employment to 300 persons and indirect employment to 500 persons. This factory, to be started in the joint sector with Japanese technology, will begin production within two years.

Tamil Nadu Road Development and Toll Collection Management Project, Tamil Nadu Toll Road Management Company Ltd. — for constructing highways, bridges and bypass roads—

The promoters’ agreement for this project was signed on 21-1-1998. As the first phase, it is planned to improve the road from Chennai Port to Ennore at a cost of Rs. 160 crore.

Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene factory. It is planned that Messrs Modi Corp of New Delhi and the Vibac company of Italy, together with TIDCO in the joint sector, will start in Tamil Nadu a project capable of producing 15,000 tonnes annually.

Vibac stands third in the world in this industry. The promoters’ agreement for this project was signed on 16-7-1997. The industrial project report is being prepared. The investment is Rs. 150 crore.

Iljin Automotive. Factory manufacturing components. For establishing a factory to manufacture parts required for cars, the Korean Iljin industrial company entered into an agreement with SIPCOT on 2-7-1997. One acre of land has been allotted in the Irungattukottai SIPCOT complex for this factory, which is to be established at a cost of Rs. 40 crore. Construction work has been completed and machinery is being installed.

Makushta Aircondition India factory. The Japanese Makushta Electrical factory and the Indian Sridhoot commercial company jointly, with an investment of Rs. 59.40 crore, entered into an agreement on 12-9-1997 to establish the Makushta Aircondition—

Marqube India factory. The Mauritius-based Marqube Asia company has come forward to establish the Marqube India factory. The agreement for this was entered into on 7-1-1998. Construction work has already begun on this factory, which is being established over three acres in the Irungattukottai industrial complex with an investment of Rs. 36 crore and 50 lakh. Mando Brake System factory. The Korean Mando Machinery Corporation is establishing the Mando Brake System factory at Irungattukottai with an investment of Rs. 30 crore. Construction work on this factory, which is being established over approximately nine acres, has been completed and machinery and equipment are being installed. This factory will manufacture car components. The agreement for it was entered into on 9-1-1998.

Optical Fibre Cable factory at Maraimalai Nagar. For starting an Optical Fibre Cables System at Maraimalai Nagar in Kanchipuram district, TIDCO entered into an agreement on 2-8-1996 in the joint sector together with the Central Government undertaking Messrs Telecommunication Consultant India Limited and Japan-based Fujikura Limited. The total estimate for this project is Rs. 40 crore. Ten acres of land have been allotted near Maraimalai Nagar for this factory. Steps are being taken to purchase the machinery required for the factory and to undertake construction work. This factory will begin functioning in the first quarter of 1999.

MCC Scrab Agro Limited coconut-processing factory. At a cost of Rs. 38 crore and 42 lakh, TIDCO, in the joint sector together with Messrs MCC Scrab Agro Limited, signed an agreement on 29-8-1996 to establish a coconut-processing factory at the village called Kinathukadavu, near Pollachi in Coimbatore district. It has been decided that coconut oil, coconut milk and products related to coconut fibre will be produced in this factory. The factory is being established over 44 acres. Production will begin in June 1999.

Next, Tauraus Novelties Limited. With South Korean technology, TIDCO has established this project in the joint sector at Hosur in Dharmapuri district together with the Bangalore-based Srivathsan and Associates company. Commercial-scale production will take place from June. Production on a trial basis has already begun.

A chicken-meat processing factory aimed at 100 per cent export. This project is to be established in the joint sector at Pongalur in Coimbatore district by Pioneer Breeding Farms with an investment of Rs. 32 crore. The promoters’ agreement for this was signed on 7-1-1998. Market conditions for this industry are being studied. The land required for the project has been selected. The project will begin production within another year.

A 100 per cent export Frozen French Fries company. Together with the Kuwait-based R.C. Suresh and Associate company, TIDCO is to establish in the joint sector a potato-processing project with an investment of about Rs. 32 crore. This project is to be established at Mettupalayam in Coimbatore district. The agreement for it was signed on 7-1-1998.
