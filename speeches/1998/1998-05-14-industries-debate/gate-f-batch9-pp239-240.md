### Source page 239

**Hon. Speaker:** Tmt. A.S. Ponnammal.

**Tmt. A.S. Ponnammal:** Hon. Speaker, for a long time I have been saying — 413 acres, 413 acres. It was during the term of the Hon. Chief Minister that it was acquired. What is there now? I was eagerly waiting for a heavy industry to come there. Because the Alanganallur Sugar Factory was brought to the Sholavandan constituency — it was brought during our period; 2,500 people are working there. I dreamed that such a situation could be created in 1999 as well. Leaving that aside, what have you put there today? In this note, it says that 380 acres have been acquired. It says that an industrial complex will be established there. In how many more years will it be established? When will it be established? You have not said how much money has been allotted for it, nor anything about employment. I consider this merely an eyewash and nothing else. It was only to say this that I asked you. I am unable to understand whether the Hon. Chief Minister has deceived me in this matter.

**Hon. Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi:** Hon. Speaker, the lady used two expressions. One was, “I dreamed”; the other was, “eyewash”. Those who wake up after dreaming naturally have to wipe their eyes. (Laughter.)

### Source page 240

**Hon. Speaker:** If Tmt. Ponnammal wished to express her views, she should have spoken on behalf of her party. It is not I who gives the opportunity to speak on behalf of the party. Next, Thiru B. Venkatasamy.

**THIRU B. VENKATASAMY:** Hon. Speaker Sir, Hon. Chief Minister was telling that the ELCOT, Hosur has earned a profit of Rs.4 crores. But I am sorry to state that the ELCOT which was started in Hosur, now converted into ELNET is now under the process of closure because of the mis-management of the Managing Director, who was posted there. So, I request our Hon. Chief Minister to rectify this. I am also giving a letter to the Hon. Chief Minister to set right the things.

**Hon. Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi:** Hon. Speaker, you mean Hosur ELCOT, do you not? What is the problem?

**Thiru B. Venkatasamy:** Hon. Speaker, they have appointed another M.D. for it.

**Hon. Kalaignar M. Karunanidhi:** Hon. Speaker, if the Hon. member gives me the details about it separately, I can immediately make an inquiry and state what the position is.
