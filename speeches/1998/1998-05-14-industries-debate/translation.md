The Tamil Nadu Steel Corporation at Arakkonam was established in 1972 as a public-sector undertaking. It was this Government that established it. The undertaking functioned in two sections. One was the iron-melting workshop — Steel Melting Shop — and the other was the electric rolling mill — Re-rolling Mill. This plant, which had operated profitably in its initial stages, later reached a very distressed condition. The reason for this was obsolete and non-functioning machinery.

In the intervening period, all these became obsolete and non-functioning machines.

Not only that, the prices of raw materials required for production increased. Maintenance was not properly carried out in the intervening period; another factor was that the production cost became higher than the selling price. All these became reasons for the plight of this plant. The plant continued to operate only at a loss. The loss in 1994-95 was Rs. 1.88 crore; in 1995-96, Rs. 5.91 crore; in 1996-97, Rs. 11.78 crore. The accounts for 1997-98 have not yet been finalised. Therefore, up to 1996-97 the total loss was Rs. 19.57 crore.

Since the Government itself fixes the price of the products manufactured by the Tamil Nadu Steel Corporation — administered price — the Government also has to provide as subsidy the difference between the production cost and the price at which they are sold. If the Rs. 53 crore provided so far in this manner is added, the total loss comes to Rs. 72 crore and 57 lakh.
