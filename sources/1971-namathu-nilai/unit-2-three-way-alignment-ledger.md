## Page conventions

This ledger distinguishes three page systems:

- **booklet scan p.** = physical page in `ACL-CPL_01726_நமது_நிலை.pdf` and the `<!-- source-page: N -->` marker in the corrected transcription;
- **booklet printed p.** = printed folio in the 1971 booklet (`printed = scan - 2` for Unit 2);
- **Official Report p.** = printed page number in the Assembly or Council Official Report, not the physical PDF page number.

## Alignment classifications

- **A-DIRECT** — the booklet follows a distinctive Assembly sequence closely enough to assign the span to the 29 March Assembly reply.
- **A-PARALLEL** — related substance occurs in both Houses, but the booklet's local ordering and transitions follow the Assembly record.
- **C-DIRECT** — the booklet follows a distinctive Council sequence closely enough to assign the span to the 29 March Council reply.
- **C-PARALLEL** — related substance occurs in both Houses, but the booklet's local ordering and transitions follow the Council record.
- **EDITORIAL-JUMP** — the booklet leaves one primary sequence and resumes another sequence after omission/reordering. Omitted primary-record matter is not missing booklet text and must not be silently inserted.

As in Unit 1, a common policy topic is not by itself sufficient evidence of source identity. The discriminator is the sequence of distinctive phrases, named interventions, transitions and neighbouring material.

## Critical boundary findings

Unit 2 is more heavily interleaved than Unit 1. It cannot accurately be described as one Assembly block followed by one Council block.

### 1. Assembly backbone: booklet scan pp.38–53

From Unit 2's opening on scan p.38 / printed p.36 through the first part of scan p.53 / printed p.51, the booklet follows the **29 March Assembly reply, Official Report pp.298–310**, in continuous order.

Distinctive anchors include:

- the East Bengal opening;
- the inventory of lawyers, doctors, teachers, engineers and graduates;
- the long sequence of opposition-party leaders;
- the Hande kitten/mouse analogy;
- Latheef's electric-fan analogy;
- the tax and registration rebuttal;
- L.I.C. / Corporation accounting;
- tractor figures;
- slum-tenement figures and `தீப்பெட்டி வீடுகள்`;
- agricultural and education spending;
- backward-class scholarship changes and colony electrification.

The Council record does not reproduce this sequence as its reply opening. Indeed, the Council reply explicitly refers to Karunanidhi having made the East Bengal statement **that morning in the Assembly**, independently confirming that the two 29 March replies are separate House events.

### 2. First switch: Assembly → Council inside booklet scan p.53 / printed p.51

The switch is demonstrated by the exact transition:

`இது இடைக்கால நிதிநிலை அறிக்கை என்பதால், இதில் எழுப்பப்பட்டிருக்கும் உரிமைக்குரல் இடைக்கால உரிமைக்குரலோ என்று யாரும் எண்ணத் தேவையில்லை.`

The booklet then immediately invokes the **Council opposition leader** and the support offered by Council members for measures to secure greater Central finance.

That sequence corresponds to Council p.201. The Assembly p.310 sequence at the corresponding editorial point instead moves into Hande's question about what the Government will do if the Centre does not provide funds.

This is therefore a direct sentence-level A→C source switch.

### 3. Council selection and internal omission

The booklet follows Council pp.201–203 through:

- all-party support for Tamil Nadu's rights claim;
- Swaminathan;
- Krishnaswami Naidu / elder-like praise;
- political violence and the warning against violence.

But it does **not** continue continuously through the next Council pages. Council pp.203–205 move through financial-allocation criteria, Chennai/slum conditions and special-fund arguments. The booklet omits that run and resumes at Council p.205 with:

- the question of what action will be taken;
- parliamentary methods;
- the `எழுச்சி நாள்` example;
- Tuticorin and Salem steel;
- the `காவடி` analogy;
- the Delhi-echo argument on p.206.

Thus the Council-derived middle is itself an edited selection.

### 4. Second switch: Council → Assembly inside booklet scan p.57 / printed p.55

Immediately after the Delhi-echo paragraph, the Council p.206 proceeds toward the State Planning Commission.

The booklet instead begins:

`அறுத்துக்கொள்ள வேண்டும் என்பது அவர்கள் ஆசை...`

and returns to the Assembly sequence concerning Ponnappa Nadar / Hande, what to do if the Centre refuses funds, policy-based support to Indira Gandhi, the 23 parliamentary votes, the Hindi warning, and the formulation:

`மத்திய அரசுடன் உறவுக்குக் கை கொடுப்போம், அதே நேரத்தில் எங்களுடைய உரிமைகளுக்குக் குரல் கொடுப்போம்`.

This is a direct C→A switch.

The booklet also compresses the Assembly source here: the longer Assembly p.311 digression on extremist/Marxist Communists, Nijalingappa Congress and electoral alignments is not imported.

### 5. Third switch: Assembly → Council inside booklet scan p.59 / printed p.57

After the Purananuru-mother / `வணங்காதீர்கள், வளையாதீர்கள்` passage, the booklet introduces:

`மாநிலத் திட்டக் குழு, போட்டிக் குழு அல்ல !`

The following ten-year State Planning Commission explanation follows Council pp.206–207, including the projected needs of population, education, medicine, agriculture and water and the expectation that the report can be discussed with the 1972–73 Budget.

This is the second A→C switch.

### 6. Fourth switch: Council → Assembly inside booklet scan p.60 / printed p.58

Council p.207, after the Planning Commission passage, continues into:

- administrative reform;
- bus-transport finances;
- pay-commission matters;
- the Council reply's formal conclusion.

The booklet does not reproduce that ending. Instead, at `உரிமைக் குரல்`, it returns to the Assembly p.312–313 sequence:

- powers must be dispersed;
- States require greater political, social and economic authority;
- Pakistan is invoked in the State-autonomy argument;
- East Pakistan is invoked;
- `மாநிலத்திற்கு மாநிலம் ரகுமான்களைத் தோற்றுவிக்க இந்தியாவிலே யாரும் முன்வரமாட்டார்கள்`;
- the Interim Budget is closed as an expression of Tamil Nadu's rights voice.

This is the final C→A switch and supplies the booklet's Unit 2 ending.

## Unit 2 source-switch model

At the defensible sentence-level boundaries, Unit 2 follows this pattern:

**Assembly → Council → Assembly → Council → Assembly**

More precisely:

1. **Assembly OR pp.298–310** — booklet scan pp.38–53 first part;
2. **Council OR pp.201–203**, followed after omission by **pp.205–206** — booklet scan p.53 second part through p.57 first part;
3. selected **Assembly OR pp.310–312** — booklet scan p.57 second part through p.59 first part;
4. **Council OR pp.206–207** — booklet scan p.59 second part through p.60 planning paragraph;
5. **Assembly OR pp.312–313** — booklet scan p.60 `உரிமைக் குரல்` through the end.

The booklet therefore combines both House replies with multiple sentence-level source switches and deliberate omissions inside individual House-derived runs.

## Material deliberately not imported into the booklet

The primary records contain material that must **not** be filled into the booklet transcription.

### Assembly omissions

The booklet does not reproduce every passage between the Assembly-derived sections. In particular, the Assembly p.311 political digression concerning extremist/Marxist Communist election positions, Nijalingappa Congress and related alliance commentary is omitted from the booklet's compressed return to the Assembly sequence.

### Council omissions

The booklet skips substantial Council material, including:

- the p.203–205 run on financial-allocation criteria, Chennai/slum conditions and special-fund questions before resuming the p.205 parliamentary-action passage;
- the p.207 post-Planning-Commission material on administrative reform, bus transport and pay-commission questions;
- the Council's own formal conclusion.

These are **editorial omissions**, not lacunae in the controlling booklet source.

## Unit 2 provenance conclusion

The correct source-critical description is:

> **Unit 2 begins with the 29 March 1971 Assembly reply as its backbone through booklet printed p.51, switches within printed p.51 to selected Council pp.201–203 and pp.205–206, returns within printed p.55 to selected Assembly pp.310–312, inserts the Council pp.206–207 State Planning Commission sequence within printed pp.57–58, and finally returns within printed p.58 to Assembly pp.312–313 for the `ரகுமான்கள்` / rights-voice conclusion.**

This conclusion is supported by Class-A Official Reports for both Houses.

It does **not** mean that either Official Report supersedes the booklet as textual authority. `ACL-CPL_01726_நமது_நிலை.pdf` remains the controlling witness for the booklet transcription.
