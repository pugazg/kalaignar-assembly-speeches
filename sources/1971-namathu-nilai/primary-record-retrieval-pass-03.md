## 2. Exact primary-document request packet — Assembly, 29 March 1971

### Requested record

**Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly Debates — Official Report — 29 March 1971**

Context:

- Fifth Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly;
- first session after the 1971 election;
- 1971–72 Interim Budget / Vote on Account;
- Chief Minister **M. Karunanidhi**, also speaking as Finance Minister;
- official Fifth Assembly review records the general discussion on 26 and 29 March 1971, with 29 March as the final discussion day.

### Phrase anchors from the controlling booklet

The purpose of obtaining the daily report is to test direct text alignment, not to replace the booklet text.

Opening anchor from Unit 2, scan p. 38 / printed p. 36:

`இடைக்கால நிதிநிலை அறிக்கை மீது நடைபெற்றுள்ள விவாதத்திற்குப் பதில் அளிக்கிற இந்த நேரத்தில்...`

Distinctive closing-area anchor from scan p. 60 / printed p. 58:

`மாநில உரிமைகளைத் தடுத்திடும் போக்கால் மாநிலத்திற்கு மாநிலம் ரகுமான்களைத் தோற்றுவிக்க இந்தியாவிலே யாரும் முன்வரமாட்டார்கள் என்று நானும் நிச்சயமாக நம்புகிறேன்.`

Other useful names/intervention anchors in Unit 2 include:

- டாக்டர் ஹாண்டே;
- திரு. பொன்னப்ப நாடார்;
- திரு. கே. டி. கே. தங்கமணி;
- திரு. கே. எம். சுப்பிரமணியம்;
- திருமதி அனந்தநாயகி;
- லத்தீப்;
- திருப்பூர் மொய்தீன்.

### Required proof before locking

A `1971-03-29` Assembly date may be locked only if the daily Official Report itself:

1. identifies the 29 March sitting;
2. identifies Karunanidhi's reply to the interim-budget discussion; and
3. supplies one or more distinctive phrase/intervention matches to the verified booklet.

The already known chronology and secondary Mujibur-Rahman phrase/date reports are corroboration only; they are not substitutes for this direct match.

## 3. Exact primary-document request packet — Assembly, 2 April 1971

### Requested record

**Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly Debates — Official Report — 2 April 1971**

Context:

- Governor's address delivered 24 March 1971;
- motion of thanks moved by **K. Vezhavendan**;
- official Fifth Assembly review records debate on 30 March, 31 March, 1 April and 2 April 1971;
- **67 members** took part.

### Phrase anchors from the controlling booklet

Opening anchor from Unit 1, scan p. 3 / printed p. 1:

`கவர்னர் பெருமானுடைய உரையின்மீது இந்த மாமன்றத்தில் உறுப்பினர் திரு. வேழவேந்தன் கொண்டுவந்த நன்றி அறிவிப்புத் தீர்மானத்தையொட்டி...`

The source immediately gives the distinctive participant figure:

`...மொத்தம் 67 பேர் இந்த விவாதத்திலே கலந்து கொண்டு உரை நிகழ்த்தியிருக்கிறார்கள்.`

Useful later anchors include references to:

- பொன்னப்ப நாடார்;
- டாக்டர் ஹாண்டே;
- கே. டி. கே. தங்கமணி;

- the final கிருஷ்ணசாமி நாயுடு / `முதல்வர்:` exchange before the editorial ornament.

### Required proof before locking

A `1971-04-02` Assembly date may be locked only after the daily Official Report directly identifies the Chief Minister's reply and phrase-aligns with the verified booklet. The fact that 2 April is the final day in the official retrospective discussion table is not sufficient by itself.

## 4. Exact primary-document request packet — Legislative Council

### Requested records

Locate the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council Official Reports covering the corresponding March–April 1971 sittings for:

1. the Governor-address motion-of-thanks debate; and
2. the 1971–72 interim-budget discussion.

A **3 April 1971** Council sitting remains a locator lead, and tertiary references to **Volume 88 / Volume 89** remain catalogue leads only.

### Important evidence limitation

The scholarly source that places a Karunanidhi Centre–State / East-Pakistan statement in the Legislative Council on 3 April 1971 cites **The Hindu, 4 April 1971, p. 1**, not a Council Official Report.

The verified booklet's Unit 2 does contain East-Pakistan material, but the distinctive formulation described by that secondary account — including the suggestion that Chief Ministers could have been invited to give their views — was not directly found in the verified booklet text.

Therefore **3 April must not be used as a text splice or canonical date unless the actual Council Official Report is recovered and aligned.**

## 5. Rejected / non-locking research leads

### `G. Vasantha Pai v. C.K. Ramaswamy` (Madras High Court, 5 July 1978)

The judgment records Vasantha Pai's later description of a Legislative Council speech by C.K. Ramaswamy on State Autonomy in which Ramaswamy allegedly urged the then Chief Minister to follow Mujibur Rahman's example if the Centre did not concede demands.

Research URL:

`https://indiankanoon.org/doc/766801/`

The judgment does **not** give the date of that earlier Council speech. Vasantha Pai's earlier Council term extended from June 1969 to April 1976, so this material cannot be assumed to belong to March/April 1971 or to either `நமது நிலை` unit.

**Classification:** contextual lead only; unusable for date, House-boundary or phrase alignment.

### H.V. Hande retrospective interview

A retrospective H.V. Hande interview confirms that Hande and Karunanidhi had frequent Assembly exchanges and that Hande was in the 1971 Assembly context.

Research URL:

`https://www.thehinducentre.com/the-arena/current-issues/article24640329.ece`

The interview does not date or reproduce the target `நமது நிலை` debates and therefore cannot lock either target sitting.

**Classification:** contextual corroboration only.
