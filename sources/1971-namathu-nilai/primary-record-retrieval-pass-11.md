## 2. Unit 1 — direct Assembly reply recovered and date locked

`927002(1).pdf` directly identifies the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly sitting of **Friday, 2 April 1971**.

The Governor-address discussion reaches the end of the Opposition Leader Thiru R. Ponnappa Nadar's contribution on printed p. **314**. Immediately afterwards the Official Report begins Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi's reply.

### Distinctive opening match

The reply opening on printed p. **314** directly matches the beginning of `நமது நிலை` Editorial Unit 1.

The Official Report has Karunanidhi address the House and refer to the motion of thanks on the Governor's Address. It then records the same highly distinctive participation figures preserved in the booklet:

- Thiru **R. Ponnappa Nadar** is identified as the final speaker before the reply;
- **67** members took part in the debate;
- although the D.M.K. had **183** members present, **33** spoke;
- the Opposition had **52** members, of whom **34** were given an opportunity to speak.

The corrected booklet source witness opens with the same sequence, including the motion moved by Thiru **Vezhavendan**, Ponnappa Nadar, the total **67**, and the **33 / 34** speaking figures.

This is no longer a chronology-only inference. It is a direct **date + House + speaker + subject + distinctive-text** match.

### Locked Assembly event

Editorial Unit 1's directly matched Assembly reply is therefore locked as:

- **House:** Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly;
- **date:** **2 April 1971**;
- **subject:** Motion of Thanks on the Governor's Address;
- **reply begins:** Official Report printed p. **314**;
- **reply continues through:** printed p. **336**;
- **formal motion completion:** printed p. **337**, where the Motion of Thanks is put to the House and carried unanimously.

`1971-04-02` is therefore no longer merely a candidate date for the Unit 1 Assembly component.

## 3. Late Unit-1 Assembly alignment — direct source anchors

The 2 April report also directly aligns a late part of the booklet that had previously been difficult to assign because it contains references to the Legislative Council.

### A. Mujibur Rahman / six-point discussion

On Official Report p. **334**, Karunanidhi discusses Sheikh Mujibur Rahman's initial six-point programme and explicitly distinguishes it from the D.M.K.'s State-autonomy position.

This directly corresponds to booklet source p. **33**, headed `முஜிபுர் ரகுமான் கோரியது என்ன?`.

### B. Reference to the Council Opposition Leader occurs inside the Assembly reply

Still in the **2 April Assembly Official Report**, Karunanidhi says that when he spoke in the Council he had referred to Council Opposition Leader Thiru Rajaram Naidu's willingness to support action for State rights.

This directly corresponds to booklet source p. **34**, which refers to `மேலவை எதிர்க்கட்சித் தலைவர் திரு. ராஜாராம் நாயுடு`.

This is an important structural warning:

> an internal reference to `மேலவை` in the booklet does **not** itself prove that the surrounding booklet text is a Council transcript.

Here the direct primary record proves that the same Council reference occurs **inside the Assembly reply**.

### C. State powers / non-secession argument

Official Report pp. **334–335** continue the same argument that devolution of powers is intended to strengthen India, not to separate Tamil Nadu.

This directly corresponds to the booklet's `இது பிரிவினைக் கோரிக்கை அல்ல` material.

### D. Land reform and bus-ceiling material

Official Report p. **335** proceeds through:

- land reform;
- the `50 சென்ட்` / house-site discussion;
- bus ceiling;
- the statement that the ceiling would come with retrospective effect.

These directly correspond to booklet source p. **35** immediately before the later prohibition section.

The Assembly report then proceeds to free bus passes, the Rs.250 question, further exchanges and the close of the motion. It does **not** proceed into the booklet's ensuing `வேடுவர்கள் சூழ்ந்த மான்` prohibition section.

## 4. Unit 1 is also a mixed editorial witness

The new Assembly report therefore changes the structural understanding of Editorial Unit 1 in the same direction already proven for Unit 2.

A substantial sequence from booklet source pp. **3–35** has direct Assembly correspondence, including very late material on State autonomy and bus policy.

But after the bus-ceiling material on source p. **35**, the booklet moves into:

- `வேடுவர்கள் சூழ்ந்த மான்`;
- prohibition in Tamil Nadu versus surrounding States/Pondicherry;
- the D. K. Shanmugam song;
- R. Krishnaswami Naidu's appeal on behalf of women not to repeal prohibition;
- Karunanidhi's final clarification that he had not said prohibition would be repealed.

That is **not the closing sequence of the recovered 2 April Assembly reply**.

Therefore Editorial Unit 1, like Editorial Unit 2, must not be represented as a verbatim one-House reprint. The booklet is an editorial compilation drawing on replies delivered in both Houses.

At present the safe classification is:

- booklet opening through the directly matched late State-autonomy / bus material: strong direct **Assembly 2 April** alignment;
- final prohibition sequence: **not part of the recovered Assembly closing** and therefore a strong Council-alignment candidate;
- exact Council textual provenance: still pending the 3 April Council Official Report.

No source transcription is changed by this conclusion.

## 5. Council 3 April reply date — official evidence becomes much stronger

The direct Council material now gives more than a secondary historical lead.

### A. Official Business Advisory programme

The already supplied Council report for **26 March 1971** records the Business Advisory programme. It explicitly assigns **Saturday, 3 April 1971** to:

1. **Reply to the debate on the Governor's Address by the Hon. Chief Minister**;
2. consideration of official Bills.

This is direct official institutional evidence of the intended reply date.

### B. Volume LXXXVIII contents

The recovered Volume LXXXVIII contents independently show that the Governor-address discussion continued on **3 April 1971**, pp. **411–456**.

### C. Newly supplied 2 April Council report

`922362(1).pdf` is the Council sitting of **Friday, 2 April 1971**. Its Governor-address pages still contain member speeches and continuing debate. It does not supply the final Karunanidhi reply in the inspected sequence.

This is consistent with the Business Advisory programme placing the Chief Minister's reply on the following day.

### D. Newly supplied 5 April Council report

`922416(1).pdf` is the Council sitting of **Monday, 5 April 1971**. Near the end of that sitting, after the Bangla Desh resolution, the Deputy Chairman reads the Governor's message acknowledging with satisfaction the Council's **Resolution of Thanks for the Address**, after which the House adjourns sine die.

Thus by 5 April the Governor-address Motion of Thanks had already been completed and communicated.

### Status

The evidence for **3 April 1971 as the Council reply date is now exceptionally strong and entirely official**:

- scheduled on 26 March as the Chief Minister's reply day;
- the bound contents show the Governor-address debate continuing on 3 April;
- 2 April is demonstrably still member debate;
- by 5 April the Governor has received the completed Resolution of Thanks.

However, under this project's strict House/date/text policy, the Council component is **not yet text-locked** because the actual 3 April daily report (pp. 411–456) has not yet been supplied and phrase-aligned to the booklet's prohibition ending.

Do not create a canonical Council transcript from the booklet solely on chronology.
