# நமது நிலை — Unit 1 three-way provenance alignment ledger

## Purpose

This ledger aligns Editorial Unit 1 of the corrected `நமது நிலை` source witness against the two directly recovered primary House records underlying it:

1. **Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly — 2 April 1971** — Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi's reply on the Motion of Thanks for the Governor's Address, Official Report printed pp. **314–336** (`927002(1).pdf`); and
2. **Tamil Nadu Legislative Council — 3 April 1971** — Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi's reply on the Governor's Address, Official Report printed pp. **434–454**, with post-reply clarifications through p. **456** (`922376.pdf`).

The controlling textual authority for the booklet remains:

- `ACL-CPL_01726_நமது_நிலை.pdf`
- Unit 1: scan pp. **3–37** / printed pp. **1–35**.

This file is **provenance metadata only**. It does not authorize normalization of the corrected booklet transcription to the wording, punctuation, spelling, ordering or interventions of either Official Report.

The existing **175 scan-supported corrections remain untouched**.

## Page conventions

To avoid ambiguity, this ledger uses three different page systems explicitly:

- **booklet scan p.** = physical page in `ACL-CPL_01726_நமது_நிலை.pdf` and the `<!-- source-page: N -->` marker in the corrected transcription;
- **booklet printed p.** = printed folio in the 1971 booklet (`printed = scan - 2` for Unit 1);
- **Official Report p.** = the printed page number in the relevant Assembly or Council Official Report, not the physical PDF page number.

---

## Alignment classifications

- **A-DIRECT** — the booklet span follows a distinctive Assembly sequence closely enough to assign its provenance to the 2 April Assembly reply.
- **A-PARALLEL** — similar subject matter also occurs in the Council reply, but the booklet's sequence, local anchors and transitions track the Assembly record; the span is therefore assigned to the Assembly backbone while recording the Council parallel.
- **C-DIRECT** — the booklet span follows a distinctive Council sequence closely enough to assign its provenance to the 3 April Council reply.
- **EDITORIAL-JUMP** — the booklet deliberately leaves one primary sequence and resumes another primary sequence after omission/reordering. This is not a missing-source inference and must not be silently filled from an Official Report.

A shared topic by itself is **not** enough to classify a span as coming from both Houses. The discriminator is sequence + distinctive phrase/intervention + neighbouring material.

## Critical boundary finding

The Unit 1 editorial construction can now be described more precisely than before.

### Assembly backbone

From the booklet's opening on scan p.3 / printed p.1 through the bus-ceiling / retrospective-effect sentence on scan p.35 / printed p.33, the booklet follows the **2 April Assembly reply as its structural backbone**.

Several themes in that long span also recur in the 3 April Council reply — notably:

- election commentary;
- post-election violence;
- State Planning Commission;
- industrial projects;
- employment;
- prices;
- Centre/State financial relations.

Those thematic recurrences do **not** justify relabelling the booklet spans as Council text. In each of the rows marked `A-PARALLEL`, the booklet's local ordering, distinctive surrounding material and transitions track the Assembly sequence.

### Defensible switch point

The first defensible source switch occurs **inside booklet scan p.35 / printed p.33**, immediately after:

`பஸ்ஸுக்கு உச்சவரம்பு விரைவில் வரும். 'ரெட்ராஸ்பெக்டிவ் எஃபக்டோடு' வரும் ...`

At this point:

- the **Assembly p.335** continues into free-bus-pass and other closing material;
- the booklet instead starts `வேடுவர்கள் சூழ்ந்த மான்` and prohibition;
- the **Council pp.451–453** supply the corresponding prohibition sequence.

Thus the switch is demonstrated by **divergence from one primary record plus convergence with the other**, not by the word `மேலவை` alone.

### Second editorial jump inside the Council-derived ending

The booklet does not simply reproduce Council pp.451–455 continuously.

After the D. K. Shanmugam / press-warning material on Council p.453:

1. the Council moves on to the **Hemavathi** issue and Karunanidhi's formal thanks/conclusion on **p.454**;
2. the booklet omits that material entirely;
3. the booklet then resumes with R. Krishnaswami Naidu's prohibition intervention and Karunanidhi's clarification from **p.455**.

Therefore the end of Unit 1 is itself an **edited Council selection**, not a continuous Council transcript.

## Material deliberately not imported into the booklet

The alignment also identifies primary-record material that must **not** be silently inserted into the booklet transcription.

### Assembly material omitted after the switch

After the source switches away from Assembly p.335, the Assembly Official Report continues with material including:

- free bus passes for legislators;
- subsequent exchanges and closing matters;
- the Assembly reply's own formal conclusion on p.336;
- the Motion of Thanks procedure on p.337.

These are not missing booklet lines. They are **unselected Assembly material**.

### Council material omitted before and within the selected ending

The booklet does not reproduce the Council reply from its p.434 opening. Council pp.434–451 include substantial material not carried as a continuous Council block in the booklet, even where some subjects parallel the Assembly-derived booklet text.

The booklet also deliberately omits Council p.454 before resuming p.455.

These are editorial omissions, not source-text defects.

## Unit 1 provenance conclusion

The earlier description "mixed two-House editorial witness" remains correct, but the present ledger sharpens it:

> **Unit 1 uses the 2 April 1971 Assembly reply as its principal backbone from booklet printed pp.1–33, then switches within printed p.33 to a selected 3 April 1971 Council prohibition sequence, skips Council p.454, and resumes the Council p.455 prohibition clarification before ending.**

This conclusion is supported by direct Class-A House records for both sides of the transition.

It does **not** mean that the booklet wording may be replaced with either Official Report. The booklet remains its own controlling source witness.
