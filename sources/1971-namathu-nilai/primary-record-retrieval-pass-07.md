## 4. Granth Sanjeevani — useful historical index pattern

Granth Sanjeevani exposes historical Madras Legislative Assembly index records in a form that directly encodes:

- session;
- individual sitting dates;
- official-report volume number;
- issue numbers.

Examples visible in its catalogue include:

- `Index to Madras Legislative Assembly Debates, First Session of the Second Legislative Assembly ... 24th, 25th, 27th, 29th, 30th, 31st May, 1st and 3rd June 1946, Vol. I (nos. 1–8)`;
- `Index to Madras Legislative Assembly Debates, Fourth Session ... 13th, 15th to 20th, and 22nd to 25th March 1948, Vol. XI (nos. 1–11)`;
- other historical records follow the same explicit date-range / volume / issue pattern.

Research collection:

`https://granthsanjeevani.com/jspui/simple-search?filter_value_2=Madras`

### Methodological consequence

These records do **not** provide the missing 1971 boundary themselves. Their value is bibliographic: they show the type of **index-level evidence** that can settle a volume/date boundary exactly.

Therefore the correct 1971 retrieval target is no longer merely “Volume I or II”; it is specifically:

> **Index / contents / title-page record for Fifth Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, Session I, 22 March–3 April 1971, Official Report Volumes I–II, including sitting dates and issue numbers.**

No 1971 Granth Sanjeevani index record was surfaced by the targeted public search in this pass.

## 5. Explicit anti-inference rule

Because Session I had 10 meeting days and two Official Report volumes, it may be tempting to divide the sitting dates approximately in half.

**That is not permitted.**

Volume breaks can be affected by page length, publication batching, issue composition and editorial/printing practice. The historical index examples demonstrate that actual sitting dates and issue numbers are the appropriate evidence.

Until an index, contents page, title page, catalogue record or primary scan establishes the break:

- **29 March → Volume I/II: unresolved**;
- **2 April → Volume I/II: unresolved**.
