## 1. Session-I Volume-I boundary is now directly established

The index bound into `713373.pdf` states:

> INDEX TO TAMIL NADU LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY DEBATES.  
> FIRST SESSION OF THE FIFTH LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY UNDER THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA.  
> 22nd, 24th, 25th, 26th and 29th March 1971.  
> VOLUME I (Nos. 1 to 5).

This directly resolves the previously blocked Volume-I end boundary:

- Fifth Assembly, Session I, **Volume I = Nos. 1–5**;
- Volume I covers **22, 24, 25, 26 and 29 March 1971**;
- therefore **29 March 1971 is in Volume I**;
- the earlier prohibition against guessing the volume break by dividing the ten sitting days remains correct, but the boundary is no longer unknown on the Volume-I side.

The exact Volume-II title/index page is still to be acquired before recording its issue-number/date list as a direct source fact.

## 2. The 29 March sitting is directly identified

The opening page of `713373.pdf` identifies the sitting as:

- Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly;
- Monday, **29 March 1971**;
- Fort St. George, Madras;

## 6. Important finding: Unit 2 is not a one-to-one reprint of the Assembly report

The direct alignment also proves why the booklet's own statement that Unit 2 is an `உரைகளின் தொகுப்பு` from the Assembly and Legislative Council must continue to be respected.

The corrected booklet witness contains passages that are **not present at the corresponding point in the 29 March Assembly pp. 298–313 sequence**.

Two especially clear examples are:

### A. Council/opposition-leader material after the labour passage

The booklet proceeds through material including:

- `## ஒருமித்த உரிமைக்குரல்`;
- `மேலவையில் எதிர்க்கட்சித் தலைவர்...`;
- additional Samynathan / Krishnaswami Naidu / parliamentary-struggle and violence material.

At the corresponding point on Assembly Official Report p. 310, the report instead moves from the labour / Central-finance passage directly into the Ponnappa Nadar / Hande exchange about what should be done if the Centre does not provide the requested assistance.

Therefore the intervening booklet material cannot be silently assigned to the 29 March Assembly text.

### B. State Planning Commission block near the close

After the Purananuru / `வணங்காதீர்கள், வளையாதீர்கள்` passage, the booklet inserts:

`## மாநிலத் திட்டக் குழு, போட்டிக் குழு அல்ல !`

with a substantial ten-year State Planning Commission discussion.

On Assembly Official Report p. 312, the sequence instead moves directly from the Purananuru passage into:

`மாநில சுயாட்சி என்னும்போது அதிகாரங்கள் பரவலாக்கப்பட வேண்டும்...`

Therefore the State Planning Commission block is also not part of the corresponding 29 March Assembly reply sequence.

### Classification rule

These unmatched passages are now securely classified as:

**not present in the recovered 29 March Assembly reply**.

They are **not yet to be labelled definitively as Council text** until the Legislative Council Official Report is recovered and aligned. The booklet itself strongly motivates that hypothesis, but primary Council confirmation is still required.
