## 1. New Class-A bibliographic narrowing — Fifth Assembly Session I

The official review of the Fifth Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly contains, in its chapter on the Official Report, a session-by-session table of publication dates and volume numbers.

For **1971, Session I**, the table records:

- session start: **22 March 1971**;
- session end: **3 April 1971**;
- Official Report volumes: **Volume I through Volume II**;
- number of volumes: **2**;
- number of meeting days: **10**.

Primary institutional review:

`https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=62e958c83cdd7afc4ea0849831fa47d394e72e17&repid=rep1&type=pdf`

### Consequence

Both Assembly targets for `நமது நிலை` are now known to fall inside a very small official-report range:

1. **29 March 1971 — Interim Budget general discussion / Chief Minister-Finance Minister reply candidate**;
2. **2 April 1971 — Governor-address motion-of-thanks reply candidate**.

Both dates lie within **Fifth Assembly, Session I, Official Report Volumes I–II**.

This is substantially more precise than the previous request for an unspecified 1971 volume.

## 2. What is and is not established about Volume I versus Volume II

The review table establishes the **session-level volume range**, not the internal break between Volume I and Volume II.

No Class-A evidence recovered in this pass specifies:

- whether 29 March is in Volume I or Volume II;
- whether 2 April is in Volume II;
- the first/last date contained in each of the two volumes;
- page numbers for either target sitting.

Although chronological ordering makes some assignments look plausible, **no date-to-individual-volume inference is promoted without direct bibliographic or scan evidence**.

Accordingly, the retrieval request must ask for **both Volume I and Volume II of Fifth Assembly Session I**, or for the date-labelled issues/pages covering 29 March and 2 April 1971.

### Important numbering caution

The CRL catalogue record's statement `v.16- Jan. 1969-` describes the serial as catalogued from its earlier run, while the Fifth Assembly review explicitly shows that the newly constituted Fifth Assembly's 1971 Session I Official Reports are numbered **I–II**.

Therefore the CRL statement `holdings begin with v.16 (1969)` must **not** be mechanically interpreted as proving or disproving the presence of 1971 Fifth-Assembly Volume I/II. Holdings staff or fiche-level inventory must reconcile the serial's catalogue chronology with the Assembly's internal volume reset/sequence.

This is now the principal CRL verification question.
