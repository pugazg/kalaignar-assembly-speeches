## 2. New Class-A evidence — official expunction chronology

The official Fifth Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly review contains a table of occasions on which words were expunged from the Assembly Official Report during the Fifth Assembly.

The earliest entries are directly relevant to this source:

1. **26 March 1971** — `General discussion on the Interim Budget for 1971-72.`
2. **29 March 1971** — `Do.` — i.e. the same Interim Budget general discussion.
3. **29 March 1971** — general discussion on the Final Supplementary Statement of Expenditure for 1970-71.
4. **31 March 1971** — `Discussion on the Motion of Thanks to the Governor's Address.`
5. **31 March 1971** — `Do.`
6. **1 April 1971** — `Do.`
7. **3 April 1971** — Tamil Nadu Slum Areas (Improvement and Clearance) Bill, 1971.

Primary review:

`https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=62e958c83cdd7afc4ea0849831fa47d394e72e17&repid=rep1&type=pdf`

### What this proves

This is **Class A institutional evidence** that the Assembly Official Reports definitely contained:

- Interim Budget general discussion material on **29 March 1971**;
- Governor-address motion-of-thanks discussion material on **31 March and 1 April 1971**.

It independently corroborates the broader business chronology already recorded in `primary-record-research.md`.

### What it does not prove

The expunction table does **not** identify:

- which speaker's words were expunged;
- whether Karunanidhi's reply falls within an expunction entry;
- the exact page on which his reply begins;
- an Assembly/Council splice inside `நமது நிலை`.

The absence of a 2 April expunction entry is also **not evidence that no motion-of-thanks discussion occurred on 2 April**. It only means this expunction table records no expunction occasion for that date. The separate official Governor-address chronology still records the discussion as extending through **2 April 1971**.

## 3. Unit 2 — 29 March candidate strengthened further

The official review separately states that:

- the Interim Budget for 1971–72 was presented on **25 March 1971**;
- general discussion was held on **26 March and 29 March 1971**;
- Demands for Advance Grants were voted on **29 March 1971**;
- the Appropriation (Votes on Account) Bill was introduced on **29 March** and passed on **30 March**.

The expunction table now adds another independent Class-A internal pointer that an Official Report exists for the **29 March Interim Budget general discussion**.

This sits alongside the already recorded secondary phrase/date corroboration for the distinctive Unit-2 sentence:

`மாநில உரிமைகளைத் தடுத்திடும் போக்கால் மாநிலத்திற்கு மாநிலம் ரகுமான்களைத் தோற்றுவிக்க இந்தியாவிலே யாரும் முன்வரமாட்டார்கள் என்று நானும் நிச்சயமாக நம்புகிறேன்.`

Two modern accounts date the corresponding Karunanidhi statement to **29 March 1971**:

- The News Minute — `How Karunanidhi pitched for greater state autonomy in the 1970s`
- Times of India — `Karunanidhi: Dravidian satrap who aced the game of coalition politics`

### Evidence assessment

`1971-03-29` is now supported by:

1. source-unit subject identity — Interim Budget reply;
2. official Assembly chronology — final general-discussion day 29 March;
3. official expunction table — Interim Budget general discussion definitely appears in the 29 March Official Report;
4. secondary phrase/date match — distinctive Mujibur-Rahman proposition preserved in Unit 2.

**Status remains: very strong candidate, NOT LOCKED.**

The missing evidentiary step is still the same: the actual 29 March daily Official Report must directly identify Karunanidhi's reply and yield a phrase/intervention match.
