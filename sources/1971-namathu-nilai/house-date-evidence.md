## Source-internal evidence inventory

### Scan p. 2 — front matter

The editorial introduction says that after the 1971 general election the Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council both met, and that debate took place in **both Houses** on:

1. the motion of thanks for the Governor's address; and
2. the 1971–72 interim financial statement.

The same front matter gives the imprint:

`சென்னை 22-5-1971.`

**Classification:** `22-5-1971` is the publication/imprint date printed by the issuing department. The page does not identify it as the date of either Kalaignar reply.

### Scan p. 3 / printed p. 1 — Unit 1 opening

The opening editorial sentence says that the Governor addressed the joint gathering of Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council members on:

`24-3-1971`

and then states that Kalaignar replied to the debate **in both Houses** (`இரு அவைகளிலும் விடையளித்தார்கள்`).

**Date classification:** the grammar attaches `24-3-1971` to Governor Sardar Ujjal Singh's address. It does **not** print `24-3-1971` as Kalaignar's reply date.

The speech then uses the generic current-chamber phrase `இந்த மாமன்றத்தில்` and reports that:

- 67 members took part in the debate;
- 183 members were present on the DMK side;
- 52 were in the opposition;
- 33 DMK members and 34 opposition members spoke.

These are strong internal contextual clues that the opening text derives from a particular chamber, but the page does not print a chamber label such as `சட்டமன்றப் பேரவை உரை` or `மேலவை உரை`, so the figures alone are not used to create a House boundary.

### Scan p. 32 / printed p. 30 — explicit `சட்டசபையில்` reference inside Unit 1

The text states:

`சட்டசபையில் இப்படி அறிக்கை வைத்திருக்கிறோம், பேசுகிறோம்...`

This is explicit source-internal evidence referring to the Legislative Assembly. However, the sentence is itself a reference to what has been placed/said in the Assembly; it is not an editorial label declaring that the surrounding source passage begins or ends an Assembly transcript at this page.

Therefore it supports Assembly provenance/context but does not independently lock a splice boundary.

### Scan p. 34 / printed p. 32 — Council opposition leader named

The heading `எதிர்க் கட்சித் தலைவரின் ஆதரவு` is followed by an explicit identification:

`மேலவை எதிர்க்கட்சித் தலைவர் திரு. ராஜாராம் நாயுடு...`

Kalaignar discusses the statement made by the Legislative Council opposition leader and comments on its meaning.

This is the first especially strong Council-specific clue late in Unit 1. It creates a **candidate House-transition locus before or around scan p. 34**, but it is not sufficient to lock a transition because:

- no new House heading appears;
- there is no date;
- there is no editorial separator;
- the wording can function as a reference to a statement made in the other House.

### Scan p. 35 / printed p. 33 — explicit `மேலவையில்` reference

The source says:

`மேலவையில் எதிர்க்கட்சித் தலைவர் அவர்கள்...`

in the prohibition discussion.

This confirms that Council material is being referred to or incorporated in the edition. It still does not say that a Council transcript **begins here**, and no date is attached.

### Scan p. 37 / printed p. 35 — Unit 1 physical close

The unit ends with an intervention labelled for `திரு. கிருஷ்ணசாமி நாயுடு (நி. காங்)` and a `முதல்வர்:` reply, followed by the printed closing ornament.

There is **no House label and no date** at the close.

### Unit 1 conclusion

The source proves that the published unit draws on replies in both Houses. It contains both Assembly-specific and Council-specific contextual references, but it never prints a secure Assembly-to-Council splice point.

The first Council-specific references around scan pp. 34–35 are useful **candidate loci only**. They cannot be promoted to canonical boundaries without outside primary evidence.

### Scan p. 38 / printed p. 36 — Unit 2 opening

The new heading is:

`நிதிநிலை அறிக்கை விவாதத்துக்கு முதல்வர் பதில்`

The editorial introduction explicitly says that what follows is a **compilation** (`உரைகளின் தொகுப்பு`) of Kalaignar's replies to the interim-budget debate in the Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council.

This wording is important: the edition represents the source as a compiled presentation of speeches from both Houses rather than as two separately labelled verbatim House transcripts.

No individual reply date is printed at this opening.

The speech itself begins with generic phrases such as `இந்த அவையில்` / `இந்த மாமன்றத்தில்`; these do not identify the chamber by name.

The opening also says that, as Finance Minister, Kalaignar had placed the financial statement before `இந்த அவை`. That is a strong contextual clue, but because the source does not name the House in that sentence, it is recorded as context rather than used as a locked editorial boundary.

### Scan pp. 38–53 / printed pp. 36–51 — no explicit House-transition marker

Across this long section the text repeatedly uses generic parliamentary language (`இந்த அவை`, `இந்த மாமன்றம்`) and discusses named party leaders and members, but the edition provides no printed separator of the form:

The absence is significant because the Unit 2 introduction has already told the reader that this is a `தொகுப்பு` from both Houses.

### Scan p. 54 / printed p. 52 — explicit Council reference inside Unit 2

Under `ஒருமித்த உரிமைக்குரல்`, the source says:

`மேலவையில் எதிர்க்கட்சித் தலைவர் அவர்களும், பேசிய மற்ற உறுப்பினர்களும்...`

This is the clearest Council-specific clue in the latter part of Unit 2.

It creates a **candidate transition locus before or around scan p. 54**, but it cannot be locked as a Council start because:

- there is no House heading or restart formula;

The text reaches its final paragraph and closing ornament. There is no late House label, no closing date, and no indication that a separately identifiable second House transcript has just ended.

## Negative finding — source-internal House split

**The edition itself does not securely expose the underlying Assembly/Council transcript boundaries.**

What the scan does establish is:

1. both editorial units draw on material from **both Houses**;
2. Unit 1 explicitly says Kalaignar replied `இரு அவைகளிலும்`;
3. Unit 2 explicitly calls itself an `உரைகளின் தொகுப்பு` from the Assembly and Council;
4. Assembly-specific and Council-specific contextual clues occur inside the continuous edited text;
5. no House restart heading, date, `உரை` label, or other editorial boundary marks an exact splice.

Because `தொகுப்பு` can involve selection or editorial blending, it is unsafe to assume that each unit consists of one contiguous Assembly block followed by one contiguous Council block merely from the first appearance of `மேலவை` wording.
