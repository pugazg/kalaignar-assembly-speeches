Research source:

`https://www.crl.edu/sites/default/files/d6/attachments/pages/sampholds-legislative07_0.pdf`

### Evidence classification

This is **institutional holdings evidence** for the Assembly Official Report serial and a materially stronger retrieval route than generic public-web searching.

It does **not yet prove** that CRL holds every subsequent volume continuously through the required March–April 1971 dates. The wording says that the Center's holdings **begin** with volume 16 (1969), but the specific 1971 volume/reel/fiche coverage has not yet been enumerated in the evidence recovered here.

Therefore:

- do not claim that CRL definitely holds the exact 29 March or 2 April 1971 fiche until CRL/OCLC holdings verification confirms it;
- do use call number `FICHE (o) 84/60200` and OCLC `54369430` as the strongest concrete serial locator currently available.

### Catalogue-heading anomaly

The CRL PDF is titled `SAMP Holdings List – Legislative Council Debates`, yet the Tamil Nadu record itself explicitly identifies the **Legislative Assembly** as author and publisher and gives an Assembly debate title.

The record-level bibliographic fields, not the generic PDF heading, control the classification of this particular entry.

## 2. CRL request mechanics verified

CRL's current lending policy states that researchers request collection material through a library's **Interlibrary Loan (ILL)** office; CRL does not lend directly to individuals.

CRL's OCLC ILL symbol is:

`CRL`

CRL also permits an ILL office to contact Access Initiatives to **verify holdings before submitting a request**.

Current service pages:

- `https://www.crl.edu/services/lending`
- `https://www.crl.edu/services/digital-services/digital-delivery`
- `https://www.crl.edu/access-crl-digitized-resources`

### Digital-delivery constraint

CRL provides digital access subject to copyright/fair-use rules. Its service pages distinguish between open-access digitization of material that is out of copyright and more restricted access where rights remain in force.

No assumption is made here that the 1971 Assembly Official Report is automatically eligible for unrestricted public digitization. The immediate research objective is **holdings verification and scholarly access**, whether by a scan supplied under applicable rules or by microform loan through a library.
