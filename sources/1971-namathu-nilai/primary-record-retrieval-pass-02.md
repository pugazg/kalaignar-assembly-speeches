## 2. Digital Assembly archive — availability versus discoverability

A November 2025 report on the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly's digital archive states that:

- nearly **30 lakh pages** of debates from **1952 onward** have been scanned and uploaded to `tnlasdigital.tn.gov.in`;
- an Assembly Secretariat official said the online coverage begins in 1952 because earlier records could not all be scanned;
- a serving MLA reported that the archive search function was **not fully operational** and that some pages were missing.

Research URL:

`https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/digitised-assembly-debates-attract-only-36000-hits-852887`

### Consequence

The 1971 target dates fall squarely inside the archive's stated temporal coverage. Failure to surface the 29 March and 2 April reports through public indexing is therefore best treated as an **index/search-access limitation**. It does not justify downgrading the official Fifth Assembly chronology already established.

## 3. 3 April 1971 Legislative Council lead — source-chain clarification

The earlier research layer recorded a scholarly secondary source, *The Domestic Roots of India's Foreign Policy, 1947–1972*, which states that the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu spoke in the **Tamil Nadu Legislative Council on 3 April 1971** about States being ignored when the Centre took important decisions, including foreign-policy decisions and developments in East Pakistan.

This pass recovered the work's supporting footnote for that statement:

**Footnote 33: `Hindu, 4 April 1971, p. 1.`**

Searchable research copy:

`https://dokumen.pub/the-domestic-roots-of-indias-foreign-policy-1947-1972-9780195611441-0195611446.html`

### Classification correction

This materially clarifies the evidence chain:

- the book does **not** cite a Tamil Nadu Legislative Council Official Report for the 3 April statement;
- it cites next-day newspaper reporting in *The Hindu*;
- consequently, the 3 April date remains a **secondary/news-derived locator lead**, not a primary legislative-record date lock.

The candidate must remain unpromoted until the actual Council Official Report is recovered.

## 4. 3 April lead — phrase-alignment test against the verified booklet

The scholarly description of the 3 April Council statement contains distinctive propositions that:

- the States were ignored in important Central decisions;
- the Prime Minister had consulted parliamentary Opposition leaders including the DMK;
- Chief Ministers could have been invited to express their views about developments in East Pakistan.

Those distinctive formulations were searched against the verified `நமது நிலை` source-witness transcription.

### Result

Unit 2 does contain an East Pakistan humanitarian opening, including the source-supported sentence beginning:

`இது வெளிநாட்டு விவகாரம்...`

However, the corrected source-witness text did **not** yield the distinctive `Chief Ministers could have been invited` / Prime-Minister-consultation formulation described by the secondary account.

### Consequence

There is **no direct phrase match yet** linking the newspaper-described 3 April Council statement to a particular passage in the booklet.

This does not prove that 3 April material is absent: Unit 2 explicitly describes itself as an `உரைகளின் தொகுப்பு`, so editorial selection or abridgment is possible. But the date cannot be used as a text-boundary marker merely because both materials discuss East Pakistan.

## 5. 29 March 1971 — new secondary phrase/date alignment with Unit 2

A separate secondary lead is materially stronger at the **phrase** level.

Two modern accounts independently date the following Karunanidhi statement to **29 March 1971**:

> `I believe no one in India would wish to create a Mujibur Rahman in each state.`

Research URLs:

- The News Minute, `How Karunanidhi pitched for greater state autonomy in the 1970s`:
  `https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/how-karunanidhi-pitched-greater-state-autonomy-1970s-151669`
- Times of India, `Karunanidhi: Dravidian satrap who aced the game of coalition politics`:
  `https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/karunanidhi-dravidian-satrap-who-aced-the-game-of-coalition-politics/articleshow/64441293.cms`

The verified booklet contains the closely corresponding Tamil sentence in **Unit 2, scan p. 60 / printed p. 58**, under `உரிமைக் குரல்`:

`மாநில உரிமைகளைத் தடுத்திடும் போக்கால் மாநிலத்திற்கு மாநிலம் ரகுமான்களைத் தோற்றுவிக்க இந்தியாவிலே யாரும் முன்வரமாட்டார்கள் என்று நானும் நிச்சயமாக நம்புகிறேன்.`

### Relationship to official Assembly chronology

The already located official Fifth Assembly review independently establishes that the Assembly general discussion on the **1971–72 Interim Budget** occurred on **26 and 29 March 1971**, with the final general-discussion day on **29 March**.

Thus three facts now point in the same direction for the Assembly component of Unit 2:

1. the controlling booklet identifies Unit 2 as replies to the interim-budget debate;
2. the official Assembly review places the last Assembly general-discussion day on **29 March 1971**;
3. secondary accounts date a highly distinctive sentence preserved in Unit 2 to **29 March 1971**.

### Status

This **materially strengthens** `29 March 1971` as the candidate date for the Assembly component of Unit 2.

It still does **not lock the date**, because the phrase/date sources recovered here are secondary and do not themselves identify the official Assembly sitting page. The repository's evidentiary rule remains: a dated canonical record requires direct primary legislative evidence and a defensible text alignment.

No canonical `1971-03-29` speech folder is created in this pass.
