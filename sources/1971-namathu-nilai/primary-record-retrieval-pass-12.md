## 2. Direct reply boundary — Karunanidhi begins on printed p.434

The 3 April report continues ordinary member discussion through printed p.433.

On printed p. **434**, immediately after Opposition Leader Thiru K. Rajaram concludes, Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi begins his reply.

The opening is explicit about both the subject and the procedural boundary. Karunanidhi says, in substance, that both Houses have debated the Governor's Address and that the **Legislative Council debate is now to conclude with his reply**.

This is not a chronology inference. It is a direct **date + House + speaker + subject + reply-boundary** match.

## 3. Immediate post-reply clarifications — printed pp.455–456

The debate does not move straight from Karunanidhi's formal closing to the vote.

Printed p. **455** contains immediate post-reply exchanges, including the exact prohibition clarification preserved at the end of the booklet:

- Thiru **R. Krishnaswami Naidu** says that Tamil Nadu has about four crore people, of whom about two crore are women, and urges that their interests be kept in mind before any decision on prohibition;
- Karunanidhi replies that he had only asked the members of the House to think about prohibition and had **not stated a definite decision**, adding that women are present not only in this State but in all States.

Printed p. **456** then contains the final Vasantha Pai amendment clarification. After the amendments are withdrawn, the Deputy Chairman puts the Motion of Thanks to the House and **the motion is carried**.

Therefore the most precise procedural description is:

- formal Karunanidhi reply: **pp.434–454**;
- immediate reply-related clarifications/exchanges: **pp.455–456**;
- Motion of Thanks carried: **p.456**.

## 4. Direct Council-to-booklet alignment — prohibition ending proven

The newly recovered report directly proves the provenance of the Unit-1 prohibition ending that had remained unresolved after the 2 April Assembly report was recovered.

### A. Prohibition as the major issue — p.451

Printed p. **451** introduces prohibition as one of the important matters raised by the Opposition and records the request that prohibition not be repealed.

### B. `வேடர்கள் சூழ்ந்த மான்` / surrounding-State comparison — p.452

Printed p. **452** contains the same distinctive argumentative sequence preserved by the booklet:

- D.M.K. / Anna commitment to the prohibition policy;
- the question why prohibition should be borne by Tamil Nadu alone rather than applied across India;
- Tamil Nadu and Gujarat as prohibition States;
- the geographical comparison with **Pondicherry, Andhra and Bangalore** at roughly fifty-mile distances;
- the image of Tamil Nadu as a **deer surrounded on four sides by hunters**;
- Karunanidhi's explicit acknowledgment that prohibition was in fact failing in practice and damaging integrity in administration.

This directly establishes the Council provenance of the booklet's `வேடுவர்கள் சூழ்ந்த மான்` section.

### C. Horse racing, untouchability and D. K. Shanmugam's song — p.453

Printed p. **453** continues through the same distinctive sequence found in the booklet:

- the practical problem of abolishing horse racing only within Tamil Nadu while neighbouring jurisdictions may benefit;
- the comparison with untouchability abolition as an India-wide policy;
- prohibition as a Gandhian policy;
- the reference to **D. K. Shanmugam** having sung a song;
- the humorous suggestion that if prohibition were repealed he might sing an even more forceful song;
- the warning that newspapers should not immediately turn these remarks into a headline that prohibition is being repealed;
- the request that members think about the issue.

These are direct text-level anchors, not merely thematic similarities.

### D. Krishnaswami Naidu / two-crore-women exchange — p.455

Printed p. **455** contains the booklet's final exchange:

- Krishnaswami Naidu's appeal based on roughly **four crore people / two crore women**;
- Karunanidhi's clarification that he had asked members to think and had not announced a definite repeal decision;
- his response that women exist in all States, not only Tamil Nadu.

The booklet's Unit 1 ending is therefore directly anchored to the **3 April 1971 Legislative Council** report.

## 5. Important structural consequence — not a simple contiguous Council excerpt

The direct Council recovery also shows that the booklet does **not** simply reproduce a continuous final segment of the Council reply.

The Council sequence is:

1. printed pp. **451–453** — prohibition / surrounding States / hunter-and-deer analogy / horse racing / untouchability / D. K. Shanmugam;
2. printed p. **454** — Karunanidhi turns to the Hemavathi issue, thanks members and formally concludes his reply;
3. printed p. **455** — post-reply clarification by Krishnaswami Naidu and Karunanidhi's prohibition response;
4. printed p. **456** — Vasantha Pai clarification, withdrawal of amendments and the Motion of Thanks carried.

The booklet selects the prohibition material from pp.451–453 and then preserves the later Krishnaswami Naidu / Karunanidhi exchange from p.455, while omitting the intervening Hemavathi/formal-closing material on p.454 and other immediate procedural material.

Therefore even within the Council-derived portion the booklet is **editorially selective and condensed**.

This reinforces the existing rule:

> do not reconstruct a canonical Council transcript by copying the booklet as though it were a continuous Official Report extract.
