## Provenance finding

The booklet is **not** a complete verbatim transcript of this Assembly reply.

The completed `../unit-2-three-way-alignment-ledger.md` establishes that Unit 2 is an editorial compilation combining material associated with both the Assembly and Legislative Council replies delivered on 29 March 1971.

At a high level:

- the booklet begins on an Assembly-derived backbone from scan p.38 into scan p.53;
- it then selects Council-derived material;
- it later returns to Assembly-derived material;
- it switches again to Council-derived State Planning Commission material;
- it finally returns to an Assembly-derived closing sequence on scan p.60.

These classifications are provenance annotations only. They must not be inserted into or used to rewrite the Tamil source transcription.
