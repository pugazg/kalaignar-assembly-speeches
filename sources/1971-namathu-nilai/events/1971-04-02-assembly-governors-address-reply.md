## Provenance finding

The booklet is **not** a complete verbatim transcript of this Assembly reply.

The completed `../unit-1-three-way-alignment-ledger.md` establishes that Unit 1 principally follows material associated with the 2 April Assembly reply through very late in the booklet, then switches within scan p.35 / printed p.33 to material associated with the 3 April Legislative Council reply.

At a high level:

- scan pp.3–35 are principally on the Assembly-derived backbone, with some subjects also repeated in the Council;
- after the bus-ceiling / retrospective-effect passage on scan p.35, the booklet changes to the prohibition sequence associated with the Council reply;
- scan pp.35–37 then contain Council-derived prohibition material and the final clarification exchange;
- the booklet itself does not reproduce the Assembly reply's complete Official Report continuation/close.

These classifications are provenance annotations only. They must not be inserted into or used to rewrite the Tamil source transcription.
