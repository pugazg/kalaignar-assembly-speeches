## 2. Legislative Council Volume LXXXVIII is now directly recovered

The front matter in `922240.pdf` identifies:

- `TAMIL NADU LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL DEBATES`
- `OFFICIAL REPORT`
- `WEDNESDAY, 24th MARCH 1971`
- `Volume LXXXVIII—No. 1`

The bound contents identify the session as:

- **Thirty-Eighth Session of the Legislative Council under the Constitution of India**;
- sitting dates: **24–26 March, 29–31 March, 1–3 April and 5 April 1971**;
- **Volume LXXXVIII (Nos. 1 to 10)**.

This replaces the earlier tertiary `Volume 88 / Volume 89` locator uncertainty for this session. For these sittings, **Volume LXXXVIII is now a direct primary fact**.

### Relevant contents-page chronology

The Volume LXXXVIII contents give the following useful ranges:

- **26 March 1971** — General discussion on the Budget for 1971–72: pp. **136–156**;
- **29 March 1971** — General discussion on the Budget for 1971–72: pp. **163–207**;
- **30 March 1971** — Discussion on the Governor's Address: pp. **230–251**;
- **31 March 1971** — Discussion on the Governor's Address, continued: pp. **270–303**;
- **1 April 1971** — Discussion on the Governor's Address, continued: pp. **314–362**;
- **2 April 1971** — Discussion on the Governor's Address, continued: pp. **369–404**;
- **3 April 1971** — Discussion on the Governor's Address, continued: pp. **411–456**.

The 3 April date is therefore no longer merely a secondary indication that the Council was sitting on the Governor-address debate. It is directly in the official Volume LXXXVIII contents. This still does **not** by itself prove that Karunanidhi's Council reply occurred on 3 April; the daily 3 April text must be recovered and aligned.

## 3. Unit 2 — direct Legislative Council reply recovered

`922282.pdf` is the Tamil Nadu Legislative Council Official Report for **Monday, 29 March 1971**.

The Council's general discussion on the 1971–72 Budget occupies Official Report pp. **163–207**.

### Direct reply boundary

On Official Report p. **200**, after Thiru K. Rajaram concludes, the Chair states that the Hon. Chief Minister will reply to the debate. Karunanidhi then begins:

`மாண்புமிகு ... மேலவைத் தலைவர் அவர்களே, இம்மன்றத்தின் முன் வைக்கப்பட்டுள்ள இடைக்கால நிதிநிலை அறிக்கையின் அடிப்படையில்...`

The reply continues through Official Report p. **207**, where Karunanidhi thanks the members and concludes; the Chair then adjourns the House.

Therefore the directly recovered Council reply is now locked as:

- **House:** Tamil Nadu Legislative Council;
- **date:** **29 March 1971**;
- **session:** Thirty-Eighth Session;
- **volume:** **LXXXVIII**;
- **subject:** general discussion on the Budget for 1971–72;
- **Karunanidhi reply:** Official Report pp. **200–207**.

This is a direct sitting + House + speaker + subject + reply-boundary primary match.

## 4. The Council reply explicitly cross-identifies the same-day Assembly reply

The opening Council reply supplies an especially strong cross-House datum.

Karunanidhi explicitly refers to the Budget debate in the Legislative Assembly and says, in substance and in the printed Tamil, that **when he gave the reply to that debate in the Assembly that morning** (`இன்று காலையில் ... சட்டப் பேரவையில் ... பதிலை நான் அளித்த நேரத்தில்`), he had referred to East Bengal and the humanitarian response to the killings there.

This directly establishes the sequence on **29 March 1971**:

1. Karunanidhi replied to the Interim Budget general discussion in the **Legislative Assembly in the morning**;
2. later the same day he replied to the corresponding Budget discussion in the **Legislative Council**.

The previously locked Assembly date from `713373.pdf` is therefore independently reinforced by a second House's official report.

## 5. Direct Council-to-booklet matches inside Unit 2

The recovered Council reply explains several passages in `நமது நிலை` that did not belong to the directly aligned Assembly pp. 298–313 sequence.

The Council reply contains strong matches to booklet Unit 2 material including:

### A. `உரிமைக் குரல்` / opposition support

Official Report p. **201** contains the argument that the Interim Budget voices Tamil Nadu's rights, followed by praise for the Opposition Leader and other members for offering cooperation in efforts to obtain greater funds from the Centre.

This directly corresponds to the booklet's later `ஒருமித்த உரிமைக்குரல்` / opposition-support material.

### B. Parliamentary method / non-violence

The Council sequence develops the point that State rights are to be pursued through **parliamentary methods**, not violent action, and connects this to the opposition's willingness to cooperate.

This is directly relevant to booklet material previously classified only as `not present in the recovered 29 March Assembly reply`.

### C. `காவடி` passage

Official Report p. **206** contains the distinctive passage:

`யார் எடுத்தாலும் காவடி தான்... எடுத்த காவடியை எப்போது இறக்க வைப்பது? அதற்கு எல்லோரும் ஒன்று சேர வேண்டாமா...`

and connects it explicitly to achieving State autonomy.

The same distinctive image occurs in the booklet Unit 2 late section.

### D. State Planning Commission / ten-year planning passage

On the same Official Report p. **206**, Karunanidhi explains that the proposed State Planning Commission is **not a rival to the Central Planning Commission**, and describes its role in planning over a ten-year horizon for population, facilities, education, medicine, agriculture and water requirements.

This directly explains the booklet section headed:

`மாநிலத் திட்டக் குழு, போட்டிக் குழு அல்ல !`

### E. Council closing

The Council reply concludes on Official Report p. **207** after further remarks concerning planning/administration, transport losses and the Pay Commission, followed by thanks to the members. The Chair then adjourns the House.

The distinctive booklet ending about `மாநிலத்திற்கு மாநிலம் ரகுமான்களைத் தோற்றுவிக்க...` is **not the Council p.207 closing** recovered here; that sentence was already directly matched to the Assembly p.313 sequence.

## 6. Structural consequence for Unit 2 — simple one-splice model is no longer defensible

Before this pass, the safest statement was that some booklet passages were absent from the 29 March Assembly reply and might be Council-derived.

That can now be strengthened:

- direct Assembly text is recovered for **29 March, pp. 298–313**;
- direct Council text is recovered for **29 March, pp. 200–207**;
- the booklet demonstrably preserves material corresponding to **both** replies;
- Council-derived anchors occur late in the booklet, including the `காவடி` and State Planning Commission passages;
- yet the booklet's final `ரகுமான்களைத் தோற்றுவிக்க...` sentence corresponds to the Assembly closing sequence.

Therefore the booklet cannot safely be modeled as:

> one contiguous Assembly block, followed by one contiguous Council block.

The edition is an **editorial compilation across the two 29 March replies**, and the source may alternate, merge, omit or rearrange material at paragraph or smaller structural level.

This is exactly why no House splice may be inferred from the first occurrence of `மேலவை` or any other isolated internal reference.

A three-way alignment ledger is now necessary:

1. `நமது நிலை` Unit 2 source witness;
2. Assembly 29 March reply, Official Report pp. 298–313;
3. Council 29 March reply, Official Report pp. 200–207.

Only that ledger can establish which booklet spans have direct Assembly matches, direct Council matches, matches in both, or editorially transformed/omitted relationships.
