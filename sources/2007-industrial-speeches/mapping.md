## Mapping and verification policy

This 2007 publication is a compilation containing multiple Assembly speeches. It is **not** one canonical speech. Each dated `உரை` is archived separately.

Structural mapping was done by direct visual inspection of the scan. OCR is not authoritative. PDF scan page numbers and printed page numbers are tracked separately. A boundary is accepted only when supported by an explicit `உரை : N` / `நாள் : ...` heading or the volume's editorial closing ornament.

The initial interface exposed only the first 150 rendered pages even though the source PDF has 329 pages. The earlier temporary note that Speech 5 was incomplete at scan p. 150 is superseded by this final map.
