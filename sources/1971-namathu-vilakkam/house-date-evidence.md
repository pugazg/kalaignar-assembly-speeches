# House/date evidence — நமது விளக்கம்

## Controlling source-internal evidence

The controlling booklet's publication introduction on scan p.3 supplies the event framework.

It states that:

1. the Government of Tamil Nadu's **1971–72 financial statement** was presented on **1-6-1971** in both Houses;
2. debates followed in both Houses;
3. Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi replied in the **Legislative Assembly on 29-6-1971**;
4. he replied in the **Legislative Council on 30-6-1971**;
5. the publication compiles those speeches for readers.

This is sufficient to establish the two underlying dates/Houses as **source-internal provenance**.

## What the booklet does not establish

The body from scans **4–60** is printed as one continuous `நமது விளக்கம்` unit. Preflight did not find a second title page, dated subheading, divider or explicit source instruction assigning a precise scan subset to one House and the remaining subset to the other.

Therefore no exact internal House splice is asserted at Gate B.

## Representation policy

- preserve booklet order as its own textual witness;
- keep the booklet-level reader entry at `date: null`;
- record the two House/date events separately as provenance;
- if Official Reports are later obtained, use them only for alignment / provenance unless a separate canonical Official Report-based speech project is explicitly authorized;
- never repair booklet Tamil from an external report.

This follows the source-separation principle already used for `நமது நிலை`.
