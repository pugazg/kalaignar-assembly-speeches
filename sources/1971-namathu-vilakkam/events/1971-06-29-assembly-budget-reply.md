# 29 June 1971 — Legislative Assembly budget-debate reply

Status: **provenance reference only**

The source introduction of `நமது விளக்கம்` states that Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi replied in the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly on **29-6-1971** to the debates on the **1971–72 financial statement**.
