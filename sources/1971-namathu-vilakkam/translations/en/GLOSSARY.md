# நமது விளக்கம் — English translation glossary

## Purpose

Controlled terminology for the booklet-only English translation. The verified Tamil controls; Assembly/Council Official Reports do not supply English wording.

## General rules

- preserve period political and parliamentary register;
- preserve source metaphors and rhetorical repetition rather than flattening them;
- use consistent institutional renderings across the booklet;
- retain source-derived names conservatively when verified Tamil alone does not securely identify an external referent;
- keep `patta` as a land-administration term rather than importing an outside legal gloss.
