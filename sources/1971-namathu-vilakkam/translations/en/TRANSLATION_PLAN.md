## Translation rules

- preserve Kalaignar's parliamentary voice, argumentative sequence, repetitions, direct address, humour, irony, metaphors and shifts in register;
- preserve headings, speaker labels/interventions and source order;
- preserve names, numerals, dates, money, percentages, units and source-printed English in meaning and position;
- do not improve factual or historical claims;
- do not translate from the Official Reports or use them to smooth wording;
- do not change verified Tamil because an English sentence feels difficult;
- where a source-derived proper name cannot be securely identified from verified Tamil alone, retain a conservative transliteration and document it in the glossary rather than importing outside knowledge.
